Sponsors

Every car MOZEE Motorsports builds is made possible by the companies below. Sponsor support funds materials, manufacturing, and travel to competition, and it puts sponsor names on a proven program: the team is a three-time World Champion, with wins in 2017, 2022, and 2024. Last year the team placed 2nd in the Hybrid Category with its car, the MP7, and 6th overall in the design competition.
