Who We Are

Mozee Motorsports is the SAE Formula Hybrid team from the Milwaukee School of Engineering. We are a student-led organization that focuses on the design, manufacturing, and competing of a hybrid formula style race car. Our team is made up of members from all engineering disciplines working together and gaining skills through hands-on applications of engineering. We engage in all aspects of automotive engineering fields, accomplishing MSOE's goals and furthering the MSOE Mindset.

  • More About the Team

What We Do

Each year we work at a high level to reach our goals of creating a functional and reliable hybrid formula style race car and competing at international competitions. To accomplish this, our team is made up of a series of subteams which each focus on a major component of the car. The subteams are split between mechanical and electrical: electrical is divided into high and low voltage, and mechanical is divided into chassis, controls, powertrain, and integration.

Competition Highlights

Our team is a three-time World Champion, with wins in 2017, 2022, and 2024. At the 2024-2025 Formula Hybrid + Electric competition, our car, the MP7, earned:

  • 2nd in the hybrid category
  • 2nd in engineering design
  • 4th in project management

Our Sponsors

Every car we build is made possible by the companies and organizations that support us. Sponsorship funds the materials, manufacturing, and competition travel that turn a student design into a running race car, and our sponsors ride along with us on the car itself, on our team apparel, and in everything we publish.

Become Part of the Team

No experience is required to join Mozee Motorsports. Students from every engineering discipline are welcome, and the team will help new members learn any skills needed along the way. Come to an All Team Meeting, any Monday at 6:00pm in S-341, and see what we are building.
